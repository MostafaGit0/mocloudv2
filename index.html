    How karaoke works

    Wat karaoke does

    Search for any song and MoCloud saves it to the Pi with its lyrics lit up word by word as it plays, an English translation next to songs in other languages, and the instrumental (tap Vocals to switch).

    Every saved song is kept on this device too, so it plays without Wi-Fi.

    How a song gets saved

    1. Search: YouTube Music, explicit versions only (no music videos or live versions).

    2. The pop-up checks the lyrics first and says where it found them (Musixmatch word by word is the best there is). If a song only has untimed lyrics, it asks before timing them by listening, with how long that takes.

    3. Karaoke it: the Pi downloads the song and makes the MP3 (about 30 s to a minute).

    4. Lyrics and translation (about 10 to 30 s more for songs not in English).

    5. It shows up in Ur songs and u can sing it right away. U can close the app the whole time: it all happens on the Pi.

    6. After that the Pi keeps sharpening it in the background: it lines up the instrumental and places every word where it's sung (about 2 min for word-by-word lyrics, around 10 min for line-timed ones). The timing gets better once that's done.

    Where the lyrics come from (free ones first)

    Musixmatch: word-by-word timing, the best there is (if it's down, the pop-up says so and which one it used instead).

    NetEase: word-by-word timing for lots of songs Musixmatch doesn't have.

    LRCLIB: lyrics timed line by line.

    Genius: untimed lyrics (then timed by listening), and the correct spelling to check against.

    Claude's web search: only when none of those have the song.

    Translations

    First: a translation made by people (Musixmatch's, or one Claude finds on the web, like Genius or LyricsTranslate).

    Else: Claude Sonnet translates it, close to the original words, with slang and dialect done right. It also fixes typos in the lyrics.

    Backups: Gemini (free), then DeepL, then Google Translate.

    Habibi: Arabic songs call the person they're sung to "him" whoever it is, so u pick her or him (in the pop-up, or the Who's habibi? button in the player) and the English follows. No AI decides that.

    Timing the words

    Whisper (speech-to-text, running on the Pi, free) listens to the song with the instrumental taken out and works out when each word is sung.

    Songs with only untimed lyrics get every line timed this way. If Whisper's words are too messy to match, Claude Haiku matches the lyrics to wat Whisper heard instead.

    Instrumental breaks are skipped, so lyrics never run on during the music.

    Where Claude comes in (and wat it costs)

    Claude Sonnet: only translating song lyrics (about 3¢ a song).

    Claude Haiku: finding lyrics and translations by people on the web, and matching lyrics to wat Whisper heard (a fraction of a cent to about 1¢).

    Never again for the same song: everything it works out is kept, so playing, checking or re-saving a song doesn't ask Claude twice.

    Background catch-up work goes through Claude's Batch API at half price.

    If Claude's credits are used up, Gemini's free tier takes over. Every use, wat it cost and wat's left is in Developer settings, under Claude.

    Everything it uses

    YouTube Music (search) and yt-dlp (downloading), ffmpeg (making the MP3), Musixmatch, NetEase, LRCLIB, Genius, Claude (Sonnet and Haiku, with web search), Gemini, DeepL, Google Translate, Whisper (on the Pi), and the song's instrumental from YouTube.

    Good to know

    Karaoke has its own space on the Pi (shown next to Ur songs).

    Songs u saved with Anything to MP4 can go into karaoke from Developer settings.

    Deleting a song removes it from every device.
